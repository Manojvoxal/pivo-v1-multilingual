Tips for best results

  • Choose the language. Pick Kannada or English above the text box or under Settings. Each language has its own voice.
  • Numbers are read for you. Dates, times, money, percent, phone numbers and units are spoken in formal Kannada: ₹1,250, 10:30ಕ್ಕೆ, 03-11-2026ರಂದು, 72%, 145 km. In English they are read the Indian way: ₹1,25,000 becomes “one lakh twenty five thousand rupees”.
  • Punctuation shapes the rhythm. Full stops and commas add natural pauses. Long text is read sentence by sentence.
  • Mixing is fine. In Kannada, write English words in English letters, like ನನ್ನ phone ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? Indian names such as Bengaluru and Hubballi are read the Indian way in both languages.
  • Emoji and rare symbols are skipped. The “How it was read” box shows exactly what was spoken.
  • Up to 1,000 characters per generation. Audio starts playing while the rest is still being made.
  • Private by design. The model runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
