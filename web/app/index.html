Tips for best results

  • Choose the language and voice. Use the two drop-downs above the text box (or under Settings): 12 languages, each with a female and a male voice. Write in the language's own script.
  • Numbers are read for you. In Kannada they are spoken in formal Kannada: ₹1,250, 10:30ಕ್ಕೆ, 03-11-2026ರಂದು, 72%. In English they are read the Indian way: ₹1,25,000 becomes “one lakh twenty five thousand rupees”. In the other languages numbers are read in English words for now; write them as words for the most natural result.
  • Punctuation shapes the rhythm. Full stops and commas add natural pauses. Long text is read sentence by sentence.
  • Mixing is fine. Write English words in English letters, like ನನ್ನ phone ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? or मेरा phone कहाँ है? Words missing from the English dictionary are read by Indian spelling rules.
  • Emoji and rare symbols are skipped. The “How it was read” box shows exactly what was spoken.
  • Up to 1,000 characters per generation. Audio starts playing while the rest is still being made.
  • Private by design. The model runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
